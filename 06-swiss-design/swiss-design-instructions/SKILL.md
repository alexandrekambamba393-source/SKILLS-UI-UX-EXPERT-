# Swiss Design (International Typographic Style): Design Instructions

## Core principle
Order through the grid. Every element sits on a visible or implied grid, and hierarchy comes from scale, weight and position rather than ornament. Clarity and objectivity are the goals.

## Grid system
1. Use a 12-column grid with consistent gutters (24px on desktop, 16px on mobile).
2. Align all text blocks, images and components to column edges. Nothing floats outside the grid.
3. Build vertical rhythm on a baseline (for example 8px). Line-heights and spacing are multiples of it.
4. Favour flush-left, ragged-right text. Avoid centred body copy.
5. Asymmetry is intentional: a large headline may sit in columns 1 to 8 while supporting text occupies columns 9 to 12.

## Typography
- One typeface family, in two to three weights. Neo-grotesque families (Helvetica-style, Inter, Akzidenz-style) are the reference.
- Scale is structural: use a modular ratio (1.25 or 1.333) for sizes. Headlines are large and tightly set; captions are small and clearly subordinate.
- Body text: 16px to 18px, line-height 1.5 to 1.6, line length 45 to 75 characters.
- Use bold for hierarchy, not decoration. Italic and all-caps only for short labels.

## Colour and imagery
- Limit the palette to black, white, one neutral grey and one accent (often red or a single primary).
- Use imagery that is objective and precise: photography, diagrams and information graphics. Avoid decorative illustration.
- Keep white space generous; it is part of the grid, not leftover space.

## Accessibility
- Body text contrast must be 4.5:1 or higher; headings may use 3:1 if large.
- Keep link and button labels explicit. Do not rely on colour alone to show state.
- Ensure keyboard focus is visible; use an outline offset from the grid edge.

## Use and avoid
Use for corporate sites, cultural institutions, documentation, data-heavy product pages and editorial work. Avoid for playful consumer products or interfaces whose audience expects warmth and decoration.

## Anti-patterns
Centred paragraphs, arbitrary spacing values, decorative gradients, more than two typefaces, text that does not align to the grid, and imagery that competes with the typography.
