# Neo-Corporate: Design Instructions

## Core principle
Professional, serious and modern. The interface must signal reliability and competence without looking dated. Restraint is the brand: clean surfaces, precise alignment, confident typography and a single assertive accent.

## Visual language
1. Neutral-dominant palette: white or very light grey surfaces, deep navy or charcoal text, one brand accent for primary actions and key states.
2. Subtle depth: 1px borders and soft, low-opacity shadows (for example 0 1px 2px rgba(16,24,40,0.06)). Avoid dramatic elevation.
3. Radius: 8px for controls, 12px for cards. Consistent across the product.
4. Iconography: a single stroke-based icon family at 1.5px to 2px weight, paired with labels for critical actions.
5. Imagery: real product screenshots, people and data visualisations. Avoid abstract stock illustration in core flows.

## Typography and density
- One sans-serif family (Inter, IBM Plex Sans or similar), with weights 400, 500 and 600 only.
- Type scale: 12, 14, 16, 20, 24, 32, 40 px. Body at 14px or 16px; enterprise tables may use 13px to 14px with careful spacing.
- Density is adjustable: default comfortable (48px rows), compact option (36px rows) for data-heavy views.

## Layout and navigation
- Left sidebar navigation for product apps; top navigation for marketing sites.
- Maintain a clear page header: title, one-line description and primary action at the top right.
- Group related controls and keep filters visible. Users should never guess where settings live.
- Use tabs or segmented controls for views of the same object, not for unrelated sections.

## Trust signals
- Show status, last-updated timestamps and error explanations.
- Use consistent naming and terminology across screens.
- Confirm destructive actions with a clear, specific message.

## Accessibility and compliance
- Text contrast at least 4.5:1; UI component boundaries at least 3:1.
- Full keyboard support, visible focus and logical tab order for complex tables and menus.
- Support WCAG 2.2 criteria for target size (24px minimum) and focus appearance.

## Use and avoid
Use for B2B SaaS products, finance tools, analytics platforms, admin consoles and corporate websites. Avoid playful motion, novelty typography and decorative gradients in core workflows.

## Anti-patterns
Too many accent colours, decorative illustrations in tables, unlabelled icon-only actions for critical functions, hidden settings, inconsistent radius and shadow values, and dense text without hierarchy.
